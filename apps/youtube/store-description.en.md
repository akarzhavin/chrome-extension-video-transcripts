Lingogram adds dual subtitles to YouTube and Netflix — the language you're
learning and your own, side by side. Understand every line, save new words in
one tap, and learn while you watch.

Works on any YouTube or Netflix video with captions. Both languages on screen
at once — you understand right away and keep watching. No pausing. No
dictionary. Bilingual subtitles (also called dual or double subtitles) turn
your watch time into language learning.

Most subtitle tools just translate. Lingogram helps you remember: replay lines
from the transcript, test your ear in Guess mode, save words as you go. You
learn from videos you'd watch anyway.


🚀 HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick your two languages — takes a minute.
2. Open any video with captions.
3. Press play. That's it. ▶️

No captions in your language? On YouTube, Lingogram fills the gap with
automatic translation. Not perfect — but you keep watching.


🧠 WHY IT STICKS

✌️ DUAL SUBTITLES
Catch new words without stopping the video. Feeling brave? Hide the
translation and just listen. Dual subtitles are training wheels — Lingogram
helps you take them off.

📜 INTERACTIVE TRANSCRIPT
Scrolls with the video on its own. Click any line to replay it. Loop a tricky
clip a few times — and suddenly you hear it.

🎧 GUESS MODE — LISTENING PRACTICE
Words start hidden. Catch the line by ear, then reveal it to check yourself.
A small game that builds real listening. Shift + G.

⭐ SAVE WORDS WHILE YOU WATCH
Tap a word or phrase to save it — together with the lines around it, so you
remember where it came from. Sign in free, and your words follow you across
devices.

🧰 MORE TOOLS
• Overlay mode — subtitles in a floating panel over the video
• Swap your two languages with one key
• Choose your caption tracks
• Works in fullscreen
• Settings sync across all your tabs

⌨️ KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
• Shift + D — dual subtitles on/off
• Shift + S — swap languages
• Shift + G — Guess mode
• Shift + O — overlay mode


🎙️ REAL SPEECH, NOT TEXTBOOK DIALOGUES
Textbooks are slow and scripted. Real videos aren't. Learn a language with
YouTube and Netflix — podcasts, movies 🍿, news, travel vlogs ✈️, Shorts,
shows. Hear it the way people actually speak.

Prepping for IELTS or TOEFL? 🎯 The hard part is listening — real speed, no
second take. Train it on videos you enjoy, and every word you miss lands in
your saved list, with the line you heard it in.


🔧 BUILT TO KEEP WORKING
YouTube and Netflix change often. When something breaks, one click on the
video reports it — and we fix it fast.


🔒 PRIVACY & YOUR ACCOUNT
Everything above works without an account. Saving words needs a free one: we
store your email and your saved words (with their subtitle lines) so they sync
across devices. Anonymous usage stats help us catch bugs — you can turn them
off in settings anytime. We never sell your data.
