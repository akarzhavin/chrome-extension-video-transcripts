Dual subtitles for YouTube and Netflix — and every new word can stay with you.
Understand a line, save the word in one tap, and build a vocabulary as you watch.

Lingogram shows your target language and your native language together on any
YouTube or Netflix video that has captions. See a line, understand it instantly,
and keep watching — no pausing every few seconds to look things up. These
bilingual subtitles (also called dual or double subtitles) turn the hours you
already spend watching into real progress in the language you're learning.

Most subtitle tools stop at translation. Lingogram is built around active
recall: an interactive transcript, a listen-first Guess mode, and one-tap word
saving turn passive watching into practice, so what you watch actually stays
with you. Your listening, vocabulary, pronunciation, and reading all improve
while you watch videos you already like.


HOW IT WORKS
1. Install Lingogram.
2. Pick your target language and a second subtitle language (a quick one-time
   setup).
3. Open a YouTube or Netflix video that has captions.
4. Press play — both languages appear together.

When a video already has caption tracks in both of your languages, Lingogram
pairs them directly. On YouTube, if one is missing it fills the second track
using YouTube's automatic translation, so you still get the dual-subtitle view.
Automatic translations aren't perfect, but they keep you moving.


WHY IT STICKS

DUAL SUBTITLES
Your target language and your native one on screen at the same time. Catch new
words without pausing to look things up. When you're ready, hide the translation
and start really listening — dual subtitles are training wheels, and Lingogram
helps you take them off.

INTERACTIVE TRANSCRIPT
The interactive transcript scrolls with the video on its own. Click any line to
jump back and replay it. Looping one short clip ("narrow listening") is how
sounds you can't parse yet turn into words you instantly recognize.

GUESS MODE — LISTENING PRACTICE
Words start hidden: catch the line by ear first, then reveal it to check
yourself. This listen-first loop makes you recall the line cold — that's what
builds listening you can rely on, instead of subtitles you only read along to.
Toggle it anytime with Shift + G.

SAVE WORDS WHILE YOU WATCH
Highlight any word or phrase in the subtitles and save it in one tap — the
selection snaps to whole words, so there's no pixel-perfect dragging. Lingogram
also keeps the lines around it, so you can review each word in its original
context. A counter tracks how many you've saved. Saving is optional — sign in
to a free Lingogram account and your saved words sync across your devices.


RELIABLE, AND PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• Works on any YouTube or Netflix video that has subtitles, including in
  fullscreen — the floating overlay keeps both languages on screen.
• Built to keep working. YouTube and Netflix change often; when something
  breaks, one click on the video reports it so we can fix it fast.
• No telemetry, no tracking. Your language and layout settings are stored
  locally in your browser. Nothing about what you watch leaves your device.


ALSO BUILT FOR FOCUSED STUDY
• Overlay mode — pop the subtitles into a small panel on top of the video so
  you can focus on the words (Shift + O).
• Instant language switch — flip your primary and secondary languages with one
  shortcut (Shift + S).
• Pick your tracks — when a video has several caption tracks, choose which one
  is primary and which is secondary.
• Fullscreen — keep your subtitles on screen even in fullscreen, with the
  floating overlay panel.
• Sync across tabs — your language and layout settings stay in sync across all
  your open tabs.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
• Shift + D — toggle dual subtitles
• Shift + S — swap subtitle languages
• Shift + G — toggle Guess mode
• Shift + O — toggle overlay mode
