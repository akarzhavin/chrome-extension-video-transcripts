# Privacy Policy — Lingogram for HDrezka

**Effective date:** June 22, 2026
**Last updated:** August 30, 2026

This page covers **Lingogram: Dual Subtitles & Transcript for HDrezka**.

Everything about what is collected, how it is stored, the optional account, the
anonymous usage analytics and how to turn them off, your rights, and how to delete
your data is the same across the whole Lingogram family and is set out in the
**[Lingogram Privacy Policy](/privacy/)**. That document governs; this page adds only
what is specific to this edition.

---

## How subtitles are obtained

To display subtitles, the Extension fetches subtitle (`.vtt`) files **directly from
your browser** to the HDrezka / Voidboost content delivery network as you watch.
These requests:

* are made directly from your browser to the platform, with no intermediate proxy of
  ours;
* contain no account data and no saved words;
* are subject to the privacy policies of those platforms.

The Extension performs no machine translation on HDrezka: it only ever shows the
subtitle tracks a title actually carries.

## The platform label in analytics

If anonymous usage analytics are on (see [Section 1c of the main
policy](/privacy/)), events from this Extension carry the coarse platform label
`rezka`. Never a video, never a URL, never a title — and never which of the many
HDrezka addresses you use.
