# Privacy Policy — Lingogram for YouTube and Netflix

**Effective date:** June 22, 2026
**Last updated:** August 30, 2026

This page covers **Lingogram: Dual Subtitles & Transcript for YouTube**, the single
extension that works on both YouTube and Netflix.

Everything about what is collected, how it is stored, the optional account, the
anonymous usage analytics and how to turn them off, your rights, and how to delete
your data is the same across the whole Lingogram family and is set out in the
**[Lingogram Privacy Policy](/privacy/)**. That document governs; this page adds only
what is specific to this edition.

---

## How subtitles are obtained

To display subtitles, the Extension reads the subtitle (caption) tracks that the
**YouTube player** already provides for the video you are watching, **directly within
your browser**.

On **Netflix**, it reads the subtitle tracks a title already carries, again directly
within your browser. No machine translation is involved on Netflix.

In both cases this subtitle handling:

* happens entirely in your browser, with no intermediate proxy of ours;
* sends no account data and no saved words to YouTube or Netflix;
* is subject to YouTube's and Netflix's own privacy policies and terms.

When one of your two languages has no track on YouTube, the Extension asks the
YouTube player for that platform's own automatic translation of a track it already
serves. This is a request to YouTube, made from your browser, and carries nothing
about your account.

## The platform label in analytics

If anonymous usage analytics are on (see [Section 1c of the main
policy](/privacy/)), events from this Extension carry the coarse platform label
`youtube` or `netflix`. Never a video, never a URL, never a title.
